# Painting your house

Select a part on the **Parts** tab (or click it on the photo with **Select**), then pick a color on
the **Colors** tab. The paint shows at once.

## How the color is shown

The app paints each part with the paint's exact color in the part's normal light, and keeps the
photo's own shadows, texture and lap lines: a wall in shade is the same paint, darker. Beside each
painted part the **Parts** tab says how close the painted wall comes to the paint chip — **ΔE**
under 1 is a difference most people can't see, under 2 is a close match.

**Texture** — painting a dark, stained wall a light color can show the old grain through as a
blotchy grey. Lower **Texture** to calm it; at 1 the photo's texture is kept whole.

## Comparing

Turn on **Before / after** and drag its slider: the photo as it is shows on the left of the line.
**Outlines** draws the parts' edges. The mouse wheel zooms; drag with **Select** to move the photo.

## Designs

Save the colors on the house as a **design** on the **Designs** tab: **Save as a new design**. A
house keeps several; click one to put its colors back on. Once you change a color, the open design
shows **changed** and **Save** keeps the change. Opening another design while there are unsaved
changes asks first.

## Saving the picture

**Save image** writes the painted photo at full size, and **Copy the color list** gives you the
parts and their paints (brand number and name) to take to the store.

> Colors on a screen are never exactly the paint — your screen, the photo's light and the paint's
> sheen all differ. Check a real sample on your house before you buy.
