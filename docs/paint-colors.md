# Paint colors

The app carries no paint colors of its own: it fetches each brand's colors from the brand, and keeps
them on your computer. Seven brands are listed on the **Paint colors** page (in the sidebar):
Sherwin-Williams, Behr, Benjamin Moore, Valspar, PPG / Glidden, HGTV Home by Sherwin-Williams and
Dutch Boy. The **Colors** tab lists the brands whose colors are here, and says how many more wait on
Paint colors.

## Loading a brand's public list

Behr, Sherwin-Williams, Valspar, HGTV Home and Dutch Boy publish their colors on their websites;
Benjamin Moore and PPG / Glidden publish palette files for designers (Benjamin Moore one per
collection, PPG one Excel file of every color), which the app finds on the brand's palette page. The
first time the app starts, it loads each brand by itself, one after another, and keeps them on
your computer — their progress shows at the bottom right, with **Cancel**. **Load automatically** on a
brand's row turns that off for the brand (its colors already here stay; **Remove** clears them) or on
again (an empty brand loads at once). **Load again** fetches a list again; its progress shows above the
list. Each brand's own website terms apply to what you download — the app downloads each list for your
own use.

## Importing a brand's palette file

If a brand moves its list or its files and loading fails (the brand's row says why), you can still
download the brand's palette file from its website yourself — Benjamin Moore's ASE files on its
"Download Benjamin Moore color palettes" page, PPG's "Voice of Colour Palette … RGB and LRV Values"
Excel file, or Sherwin-Williams' and Behr's palette downloads. On the brand's row press **Import a
file…** and choose the file. The app reads Adobe swatch files
(.ase, .aco), Excel (.xlsx) and CSV. How big a file may be is in **App Settings → General**.

**Remove** takes a brand's colors off this computer; it asks first. Designs that use them show no
color until the brand is added again.

## How colors are named

Each color is shown the way its brand writes it — "SW 7008 Alabaster". Just House Paint is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any paint brand; the brands' names and color names belong to them.

## Finding a color

On a house's **Colors** tab: choose the brand, search by name or number, and click a color to paint
the selected part. **Order** lists the colors by **Color**, as a paint store's color wall — whites,
grays and blacks first, then pinks and reds, oranges, yellows, greens, blues and purples, each from
light to dark — or by **Name**; the app keeps your choice. **Color family** shows only the
**Neutrals** (whites, grays, blacks and the grayest beiges) or one of the hue families. The families
and where each begins are in **Settings → Advanced** ("How colors are ordered by color").
**Exterior colors only** hides the colors a brand marks for inside use (colors
it doesn't mark either way stay). **An exact color** — a hex color, or one from the picker — finds
the brand's nearest paints, closest first, each with its ΔE.

**A color from the photo**: choose **Pick a color** above the photo and click it — the color there
goes into **An exact color**, with the brand's nearest paints. On the **Parts** tab, the small round
swatch beside a part is its color as photographed; click it to find the paints nearest the color
your house has now. Light and shadow are in a photo's colors, so the nearest paint is close, not
exact — check a chip against the wall.

## Discontinued colors

Some lists include colors a brand no longer sells. They are hidden unless you turn on **Show
discontinued colors** — useful for matching the paint already on your house.
