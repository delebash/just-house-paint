# Paint colors

The app comes with no paint colors — you add the brands you want on the **Paint colors** page (in
the sidebar). Seven brands are listed: Sherwin-Williams, Behr, Benjamin Moore, Valspar,
PPG / Glidden, HGTV Home by Sherwin-Williams and Dutch Boy.

## Loading a brand's public list

Behr, Sherwin-Williams, Valspar, HGTV Home and Dutch Boy publish their colors on their websites.
**Load the colors** downloads that list once and keeps it on your computer; **Load again** fetches
it again. Its progress shows above the list while it runs, with **Cancel**. Each brand's own website terms apply to what you
download — the app downloads only when you press the button, and only for your own use.

## Importing a brand's palette file

Benjamin Moore and PPG / Glidden publish palette files for designers instead:

- **Benjamin Moore** — the ASE files on its "Download Benjamin Moore color palettes" page, one per
  collection (Classic Colors, Color Preview, Historical Colors, …). Import each collection you want.
- **PPG / Glidden** — the Excel file ("Voice of Colour Palette … RGB and LRV Values") on PPG's
  palette downloads page.

On the brand's row press **Import a file…** and choose the file. The app reads Adobe swatch files
(.ase, .aco), Excel (.xlsx) and CSV. How big a file may be is in **App Settings → General**.

**Remove** takes a brand's colors off this computer; it asks first. Designs that use them show no
color until the brand is added again.

## How colors are named

Each color is shown the way its brand writes it — "SW 7008 Alabaster". Just House Paint is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any paint brand; the brands' names and color names belong to them.

## Finding a color

On a house's **Colors** tab: choose the brand, search by name or number, and click a color to paint
the selected part. **Exterior colors only** hides the colors a brand marks for inside use (colors
it doesn't mark either way stay). **An exact color** — a hex color, or one from the picker — finds
the brand's nearest paints, closest first, each with its ΔE.

**A color from the photo**: choose **Pick a color** above the photo and click it — the color there
goes into **An exact color**, with the brand's nearest paints. On the **Parts** tab, the small round
swatch beside a part is its color as photographed; click it to find the paints nearest the color
your house has now. Light and shadow are in a photo's colors, so the nearest paint is close, not
exact — check a chip against the wall.

## Discontinued colors

Some lists include colors a brand no longer sells. They are hidden unless you turn on **Show
discontinued colors** — useful for matching the paint already on your house.
